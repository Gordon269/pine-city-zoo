ELEPHANTS

Elephants are large mammals of the family Elephantidae and the order Proboscidea. Two are scattered throughout sub-Saharan Africa, South Asia, and Southeast Asia.

All elephants have several distinctive features the most notable of which is a long tusks, which can serve as weapons and as tools for moving objects, lifting water and grasping objects.Elephants large ear flaps controls their body temperature.Their pillar-like legs can carry their great weight.

Elephants are herbivorous and can be found in different habitats including savannas, forests, desserts and marshes. They prefer to stay close to water.
